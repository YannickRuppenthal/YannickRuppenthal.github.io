Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Galatea is a personal app operated by one person, Yannick Ruppenthal, for his own use. The only Google account that signs in to it is his own.

What data the app accesses

With the permission he grants when signing in, the app accesses his Google Calendar events, Google Tasks and, if he allows it, Gmail messages (read) and Gmail drafts (create and edit; the app never sends mail).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show and help manage his own schedule, to-dos and mail. It is stored on his own computer, and the sign-in token is kept in a local file there. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. Text from it may be sent to the AI model provider he configures in order to answer his own requests, under that provider's terms.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by choosing Disconnect in the app, which deletes the stored token.
