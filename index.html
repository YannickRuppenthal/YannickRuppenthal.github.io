Galatea

Galatea is a personal, single-user companion and assistant app that its author, Yannick Ruppenthal, runs on his own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

The app asks for access to the author's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks (and optionally Gmail drafts) so that its built-in assistant can read and help manage his schedule and to-do lists.
